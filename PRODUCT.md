# Handdown
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Supervisors and learners in small assembly workshops, sharing practical expertise at a fixed training bench.
## Product Purpose
Turn an expert demonstration into a reviewed, reusable lesson and support guided practice with explicit human help.
## Operating Context
One harmless tabletop display stand, one workspace, three intended visual checkpoints, desktop Chrome. The user confirmed that the lesson workspace should define this redesign.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Current preview: local drafts, practice acknowledgements, photo uploads, review annotations and session records. Firebase, local model inference, actual approval permissions and offline packaging are not yet configured. Never present simulated AI passes or local review annotations as authenticated decisions.
## Brand Commitments
Name: Handdown. Development specification: light neutral background, readable dark text, restrained status colors. Workstation precision and simple recovery. User requested a fresh UI using Impeccable.
## Evidence on Hand
Supplied development specification and a seeded example lesson. No actual workshop photos, measured evaluation results or customer usage statistics.
## Product Principles
Keep the next action clear. Preserve privacy boundaries. Separate human acknowledgements from automated results. Preserve work when operations fail.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard actions, readable body text at least 16px, high contrast, visible focus, controls at least 44px.
